About Barangay Bulsa-Korapot Document Request System

The Barangay Bulsa-Korapot Document Request System is a comprehensive digital portal designed to automate local government service delivery. It allows residents to request official documents like Barangay Clearances and Certificates remotely while enforcing multi-layered identity verification. Developed strictly as a school project requirement and presentation prototype for our BSIT program.

Online Document Application
Enables residents to request Barangay Clearances, Certificates of Residency, and Indigency remotely without physical queuing.
Automated Tesseract OCR
Integrates Tesseract.js to automatically analyze uploaded IDs (PhilSys, School ID, Driver's License, UMID) for validity verification.
Biometric Liveness Scan
Powered by MediaPipe FaceMesh for 3-step real-time head landmark tracking to prevent proxy and fraudulent submissions.
Telegram Alerts & OTP Bot
Dispatches instant request approval/rejection alerts and 6-digit registration OTP codes directly via @BrgyDocRequestBot.
Embedded AI Assistant
Features a bilingual (Tagalog/English) AI engine to answer queries regarding clearances, requirements, and portal navigation.
Admin & Anti-Spam Controls
Equips barangay officials with verification review tools, user account controls, and automated anti-spam click protection.
